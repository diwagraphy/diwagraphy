"I don't chase perfect light. I chase the moment right before someone stops noticing the camera."

I shoot a mix of everything — quiet streets, long roads, and the faces of people who let me stay a little longer than a stranger should. Most of what matters in a photograph never shows up in the frame, so I write it on the back instead, the way my grandfather used to.

Currently shooting

A slow, ongoing project across coastal towns and holy cities — collecting the stories people tell about the places they call home, one roll at a time.
