About Us

The story behind the lens.

Diwagraphy is a photography portfolio and journal — a running record of frames, and the small true stories that sat just outside them.

"I don't chase perfect light. I chase the moment right before someone stops noticing the camera."

Every photograph, caption, and journal entry on this site is shot and written by one person. Diwagraphy isn't a studio or a team — it's one photographer's ongoing roll, developed a little at a time and shared here as it comes in.
